# ⛵ Peterwolf's Minecraft Boats & Ships

**Peterwolf's Boats & Ships** adds three original, steerable wooden watercraft to Minecraft with custom 3D models, 512x512 HD pixel-art textures, server-authoritative physics, and dynamic client-side animations.

---

## 🚤 Features & Ships

### 🚣 1. River Skiff (Łódź Wiosłowa)
* **Seats**: 2 Players / Mobs
* **Features**: Fast and nimble river craft equipped with side-extending oars anchored in iron rowlocks.
* **Animation**: Oars remain still at rest, and sweep back & forth while dipping deep underwater when rowing.

### ⛵ 2. Explorer Sloop (Sloop Badawczy)
* **Seats**: 4 Passengers
* **Cargo**: 9-Slot Storage Chest on deck
* **Features**: Single-mast explorer with a cream sail, stern helm wheel, bowsprit, and lantern.
* **Animation**: Sails automatically roll up / furl under the yardarm when stationary, and unfurl gracefully when sailing.

### 🛳️ 3. Merchant Schooner (Szkuner Handlowy)
* **Seats**: 6 Passengers
* **Cargo**: 27-Slot Large Storage Hold (Crates & Barrels)
* **Features**: Double-mast cargo vessel with a quarterdeck cabin, stern lantern, and dual helm.
* **Animation**: Dual cream sails furl when idle and catch the wind when underway.

---

## ⚙️ Technical & Gameplay Highlights

- **Server-Authoritative Movement**: Smooth WASD controls with realistic inertia, turn rates, and hull heel tilt.
- **Dry Deck Floors**: Inner floor floating heights calculated so water never clips through decks.
- **Enclosed Hulls**: Solid bow, stern, and gunwale walls with 512x512 UV texture maps.
- **Survival Ready**: Fully craftable in survival mode with custom crafting recipes and creative tab integration.
